Details of Historic Places

Lalbagh Fort

Location: Dhaka, Bangladesh

Era: Mughal Period

Lalbagh Fort is one of the most famous Mughal historical sites in Bangladesh. It was built in the 17th century. The fort is known for its beautiful architecture, mosque, tomb and garden.

Paharpur / Somapura Mahavihara

Location: Naogaon, Bangladesh

Era: Ancient Buddhist Period

Paharpur is an important archaeological site in Bangladesh. It is also known as Somapura Mahavihara. It was a famous Buddhist monastery and learning centre in ancient Bengal.

Mahasthangarh

Location: Bogura, Bangladesh

Era: Ancient Period

Mahasthangarh is one of the oldest archaeological sites in Bangladesh. It contains the remains of an ancient city. It is important for understanding the early history and civilization of Bengal.

Sixty Dome Mosque

Location: Bagerhat, Bangladesh

Era: Sultanate Period

The Sixty Dome Mosque is a famous mosque in Bagerhat. It is one of the most important Islamic architectural sites in Bangladesh. It shows the beauty of medieval Muslim architecture.

Forest Ecosystems Around The World

Explore different forest ecosystems and learn about nature.

Tropical Forest

Tropical forests are hot and rainy forests found near the equator.

Key Facts:

  • High rainfall
  • Rich biodiversity
  • Dense trees

Temperate Forest

Temperate forests experience four seasons and moderate rainfall.

Key Facts:

  • Moderate climate
  • Four seasons
  • Deciduous trees

Boreal Forest

Boreal forests are cold forests found in northern regions.

Key Facts:

  • Cold climate
  • Conifer trees
  • Long winters

Mangrove Forest

Mangrove forests grow in coastal areas with salty water.

Key Facts:

  • Found near sea coasts
  • Salt tolerant trees
  • Protects coastline

Mediterranean Forest

Mediterranean forests have hot dry summers and mild winters.

Key Facts:

  • Warm climate
  • Dry summers
  • Shrubs and oak trees

Montane Forest

Montane forests are found in mountain regions.

Key Facts:

  • Cool temperature
  • Mountain ecosystem
  • Rich wildlife

Cloud Forest

Cloud forests are covered with fog and clouds most of the time.

Key Facts:

  • Very humid
  • High rainfall
  • Located on mountains

Dry Forest

Dry forests receive low rainfall and have dry seasons.

Key Facts:

  • Low rainfall
  • Dry climate
  • Drought resistant trees

Snacks

Fuchka

৳ 40 - 80

★★★★☆

Prep Time: 20 mins

Ingredients

  • Fuchka shells
  • Mashed potatoes
  • Chickpeas
  • Tamarind water
  • Spices

Instructions

  1. Mix potatoes and chickpeas.
  2. Add spices.
  3. Fill shells.
  4. Serve with tamarind water.

Sweets

Jilapi

৳ 10 - 20

★★★★☆

Prep Time: 35 mins

Ingredients

  • All-purpose flour
  • Yogurt
  • Baking soda
  • Sugar syrup
  • Oil for frying

Instructions

  1. Mix flour, yogurt, and baking soda into a batter.
  2. Pour batter into hot oil in spiral shapes.
  3. Fry until golden brown.
  4. Dip into sugar syrup and serve warm.

Chomchom

৳ 30 - 50

★★★★☆

Prep Time: 35 mins

Ingredients

  • Milk powder
  • Flour
  • Sugar syrup
  • Cardamom
  • Grated coconut

Instructions

  1. Prepare dough using milk powder and flour.
  2. Shape into oval balls and fry lightly.
  3. Boil in sugar syrup with cardamom.
  4. Coat with grated coconut before serving.

Roshmalai

৳ 30 - 100

★★★★★

Prep Time: 35 mins

Ingredients

  • Milk
  • Chana balls
  • Sugar
  • Cardamom
  • Pistachios

Instructions

  1. Boil milk until slightly thick.
  2. Add sugar and cardamom.
  3. Place chana balls into the milk.
  4. Garnish with pistachios and chill before serving.

Akher Sorbot

৳ 20 - 30

★★★★☆

Prep Time: 35 mins

Ingredients

  • Sugarcane juice
  • Lemon juice
  • Black salt
  • Mint leaves
  • Ice cubes

Instructions

  1. Pour fresh sugarcane juice into a glass.
  2. Add lemon juice and black salt.
  3. Mix well with mint leaves.
  4. Serve cold with ice cubes.

Lassi

৳ 30 - 120

★★★★★

Prep Time: 10 mins

Ingredients

  • Yogurt
  • Cold water or milk
  • Sugar
  • Ice cubes
  • Cardamom powder

Instructions

  1. Add yogurt, sugar, and milk into a blender.
  2. Blend until smooth and creamy.
  3. Add ice cubes and cardamom powder.
  4. Pour into a glass and serve chilled.
